### Handling Bioassemblies and Chemical Components
AlphaFold 3 represents and manages molecular structures, including specialized handling for biological assembly information and chemical component data. The data structures and parsing mechanisms for biological assemblies are defined in [`/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/structure/bioassemblies.py`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/structure/bioassemblies.py). The [`BioassemblyData`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/structure/bioassemblies.py#L84) class stores and processes information related to macromolecular complexes from mmCIF files. It utilizes [`Operation`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/structure/bioassemblies.py#L32) dataclasses to represent individual rigid transformations (rotation and translation) and [`Transform`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/structure/bioassemblies.py#L44) dataclasses to define sequences of these operations that are applied to specific chains. The [`BioassemblyData.from_mmcif`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/structure/parsing.py#L293) method parses this information from an [`mmcif.Mmcif`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/structure/parsing.py#L171) object, extracting details such as [`_pdbx_struct_assembly_gen`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/structure/bioassemblies.py#L96) to determine how different chains are transformed to form an assembly. The [`get_transforms`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/structure/bioassemblies.py#L231) method then generates a sequence of [`Transform`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/structure/bioassemblies.py#L44) objects, handling chain ID renaming to ensure unique identifiers for all transformed copies of chains within an assembly. This allows for the reconstruction of full biological assemblies from the asymmetric unit coordinates.

Similarly, AlphaFold 3 incorporates robust management of chemical component data, which describes the properties of individual chemical compounds, including their atoms and bonds. This functionality is primarily implemented in [`/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/structure/chemical_components.py`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/structure/chemical_components.py). The core data structures include [`ChemCompAtom`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/structure/chemical_components.py#L47) and [`ChemCompBond`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/structure/chemical_components.py#L72), which represent atoms and bonds within a chemical component, respectively. These are aggregated into [`ChemCompEntry`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/structure/chemical_components.py#L93) objects, which encapsulate all information for a single chemical component, such as its name, formula, and SMILES string. A collection of these entries is managed by the [`ChemicalComponentsData`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/structure/chemical_components.py#L142) class. The [`ChemicalComponentsData.from_mmcif`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/structure/parsing.py#L299) method parses chemical component definitions directly from mmCIF files, while [`to_mmcif_dict`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/structure/structure.py#L2952) facilitates serialization back into mmCIF format. This module can also retrieve and populate missing chemical component data from a Chemical Components Dictionary (CCD), ensuring comprehensive representation of both standard and non-standard chemical entities within a structure. This detailed management of both bioassembly and chemical component data enables a comprehensive understanding and manipulation of complex molecular systems. For more on the foundational data structures of molecules, refer to [Core Data Structures for Molecular Representation](#molecular-structure-management-core-data-structures-for-molecular-representation).
