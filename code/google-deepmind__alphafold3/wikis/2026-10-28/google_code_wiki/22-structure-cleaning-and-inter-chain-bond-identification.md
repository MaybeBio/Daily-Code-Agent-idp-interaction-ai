#### Structure Cleaning and Inter-Chain Bond Identification
Raw [`structure.Structure`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/run_alphafold_data_test.py#L88) objects undergo a cleaning process within the data pipeline to prepare them for featurization and model input. This involves removing elements not relevant to the modeling task or those that could introduce noise. The [`clean_structure`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/model/pipeline/structure_cleaning.py#L75) function in [`/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/model/pipeline/structure_cleaning.py`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/model/pipeline/structure_cleaning.py) orchestrates these operations. This includes filtering out non-standard atoms, water molecules, and hydrogen atoms. Chains without specified sequences can also be dropped. A more complex cleaning step involves the removal of "leaving atoms," such as those associated with amino acid termini (e.g., OXT, HXT) or specific atoms in glycans, to ensure the structural representation is consistent. The process can also enforce the use of only covalent bonds and identify and remove "bad bonds" based on distance thresholds or polymer-polymer interactions.

Following structure cleaning, the pipeline identifies and processes inter-chain bonds, which are critical for understanding how different molecular components interact. The module [`/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/model/pipeline/inter_chain_bonds.py`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/model/pipeline/inter_chain_bonds.py) provides functions like [`get_polymer_ligand_bonds`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/model/pipeline/inter_chain_bonds.py#L171) and [`get_ligand_ligand_bonds`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/model/pipeline/inter_chain_bonds.py#L135) to specifically extract bonds between polymers and ligands, and between different ligands, respectively. These functions filter bonds based on criteria such as chain type, residue name, bond type, and maximum bond distance. The bond information is then represented in an [`atom_layout.AtomLayout`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/run_alphafold_data_test.py#L93) object, detailing the atoms involved in each bond. If configured, the system also handles cases where an atom might be involved in multiple bonds, ensuring a consistent representation. The overall process, managed by the [`WholePdbPipeline`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/model/pipeline/pipeline.py#L86) in [`/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/model/pipeline/pipeline.py`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/model/pipeline/pipeline.py), consolidates these cleaned structures and identified bonds with other generated features into a [`feat_batch.Batch`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold3/src/alphafold3/model/model.py#L80) object, which is then ready for model consumption. For further details on the broader context of data processing, refer to the [Macromolecular Data Processing Pipeline](#core-model-architecture-and-pipeline-macromolecular-data-processing-pipeline) section.
