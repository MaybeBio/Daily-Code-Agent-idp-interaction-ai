# Analysis notebooks

This directory contains Jupyter notebooks used to analyse and visualise results presented in the manuscript.

## Directory contents

- **`Dataset_analysis/`** – Analysis and visualisation of the literature-derived HX-NMR protection factor dataset.
- **`Forward_models/`** – Benchmarking and comparison of empirical and explicit open-state HX forward models, including structural descriptors and electrostatic corrections.
- **`descriptor_redundancy/`** – Analysis of redundancy and overlap between structural descriptors used to identify exchange-competent amides.
- **`PH_model_comparison_peptides/`** – Evaluation of the Persson–Halle (PH) explicit-water model and other open-state models using short peptides.
- **`IDP_HDXrew_cross_validation/`** – HX-guided ensemble reweighting of intrinsically disordered proteins and cross-validation against independent NMR and SAXS measurements.
- **`Folded_HDXrew_cross_validation/`** – HX-guided reweighting of folded-protein ensembles and cross-validation against independent structural and dynamical observables.
- **`Folding_landscapes/`** – Analysis and HX-guided reweighting of BioEmu-generated folding landscapes, including validation against experimental folding free energies.
- **`DESRES_FOLDING_analysis/`** – Analysis of long-timescale folding and unfolding MD trajectories and HX-guided refinement of folded-state populations.
- **`Excited_states/`** – Characterisation of folding intermediates and low-population excited conformational states in HX-reweighted ensembles.
- **`BPTI_DESRES_native_analysis/`** – Analysis of the millisecond DESRES BPTI trajectory, including disulfide conformational states and local unfolding events.
- **`Example_plots_HDX/`** – Notebooks generating representative HX predictions, comparisons and visualisations used in the manuscript.
