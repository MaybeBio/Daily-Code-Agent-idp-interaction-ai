### Amber Relaxation and Energy Minimization
The AlphaFold pipeline incorporates Amber relaxation to refine predicted protein structures, addressing structural issues and improving accuracy. This iterative process uses OpenMM for energy minimization, harmonic restraints to maintain structural integrity, and identifies and resolves structural violations.

The relaxation process begins by cleaning up input protein structures using a combination of [`pdbfixer`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold/pyproject.toml#L49) and custom logic in [`/google-deepmind/alphafold/alphafold/relax/cleanup.py`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold/alphafold/relax/cleanup.py). This step handles issues such as nonstandard residues, heterogens, and missing atoms, ensuring the structure is suitable for energy minimization. The [`cleanup.fix_pdb`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold/alphafold/relax/cleanup_test.py#L51) function applies standard [`pdbfixer`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold/pyproject.toml#L49) operations, while [`cleanup.clean_structure`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold/alphafold/relax/amber_minimize.py#L182) performs specialized fixes like correcting Selenium in Methionine residues.

The core of the relaxation is managed by the functions within [`/google-deepmind/alphafold/alphafold/relax/amber_minimize.py`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold/alphafold/relax/amber_minimize.py). It orchestrates an iterative pipeline where an input protein undergoes multiple rounds of energy minimization via [`run_pipeline`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold/alphafold/relax/amber_minimize.py#L446). During each round, [`_openmm_minimize`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold/alphafold/relax/amber_minimize.py#L79) configures OpenMM with an Amber force field and applies harmonic restraints to specific atoms using [`_add_restraints`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold/alphafold/relax/amber_minimize.py#L49) to prevent excessive deviation from the initial structure. The strength of these restraints is controlled by a [`stiffness`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold/run_alphafold.py#L691) parameter.

Following minimization, the structure is analyzed for violations, such as clashes or improbable bond lengths, using the [`find_violations`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold/alphafold/relax/amber_minimize.py#L333) function in [`/google-deepmind/alphafold/alphafold/relax/amber_minimize.py`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold/alphafold/relax/amber_minimize.py). Residues involved in these violations are identified and subsequently excluded from restraints in future minimization steps within the current iteration. This adaptive approach allows problematic regions of the protein to relax more freely, improving the overall quality of the structure while maintaining the integrity of well-folded regions. The relaxation process continues until a specified tolerance is met or a maximum number of iterations is reached.

The [`AmberRelaxation`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold/alphafold/relax/relax.py#L23) class in [`/google-deepmind/alphafold/alphafold/relax/relax.py`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold/alphafold/relax/relax.py) provides the primary interface for this process. It takes a protein structure, executes the Amber minimization pipeline via its [`process`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphafold/alphafold/relax/relax.py#L60) method, and returns the refined structure along with debugging information, including energy changes and RMSD. This ensures that the final predicted protein structures are chemically plausible and physically realistic.
